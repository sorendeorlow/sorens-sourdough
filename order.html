How it works

Pre-order, 48 hours ahead. Every loaf is baked the morning of your pickup. No shelf inventory, no leftovers — yours is yours.

Pickup, 3–6 PM. From our home kitchen on your selected date. We'll confirm the exact window when we respond.

Cash or Venmo at pickup.
