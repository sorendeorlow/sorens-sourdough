The Line · Brödet

Communion Breads

Bread made for the table — for tearing, sharing, gathering. Each loaf is naturally leavened with our own levain, fermented for 24–36 hours, and baked one-at-a-time in a 6-quart cast iron Dutch oven. Available as full loaves (≈1kg) or half loaves (≈500g).
